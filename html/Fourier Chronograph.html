How this dial works

Any closed loop can be redrawn by chaining rotating arrows. Each circle is one harmonic: it turns a whole number of times per cycle, and its arm is pinned to the end of the one before. At Sync tempo a cycle is one minute, so the tip sweeps the whole shape between :00 and :59.

The twelve largest circles are the anchors. A thread ties each one to an hour numeral, and the direction its arm points lights a tick on the minute track. Lower the circle count to watch detail fall away. Fit compares the circles with the original loop, and 100% means a perfect redraw.

Time ladder

Every column is one fifth of the column before it. Select any cell to start a countdown for that span. The timer joins the others in the dock and draws a ring on the dial.

Years count as 365 days. Cell shading follows each span's length on a log scale.
